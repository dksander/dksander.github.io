For me, conference season is something I always look forward to.

It's a chance to get away from everyday work, learn from some of the best in the industry and come home with new knowledge and inspiration.

A lot is happening in the Business Central world, and the pace has only picked up in recent years.

When I started in the industry, I was handed a manual, *Microsoft C/SIDE Development 2013 R2*, and told:

> "If you press F1 in the development environment, it tells you how to code."

That's probably not the onboarding you would offer new developers today 😉 Luckily.

Even though it worked in its own way, I also think we as an industry missed out on a lot of fantastic developers, consultants and other talented people, because the way into the NAV world could simply be quite steep.

Over time, forums and blogs got better and better, and Microsoft has really lifted its documentation since. Today, Microsoft Learn is actually one of the places I start when I need to find information.

Even back then, I looked to the conferences and the speakers. That was where you had to go if you wanted the latest knowledge from Microsoft and to learn from some of the people at the very front of the NAV world.

This year I'm lucky enough that Directions EMEA will be my fifth, and I'm really looking forward to it 😊

My focus this time will especially be AI and development.

A huge amount is happening in both areas right now, and I look forward to seeing what I can bring home and use in practice.

Maybe we'll see each other there 😉
